About this demo

Kaldora Facility Services is a fictional facility-services company in Graz, Austria, with four service lines (Cleaning Services, Technical Maintenance, Security Services, Catering) and two support departments (Administration, Sales & Marketing). The finance team wanted one management P&L instead of the monthly spreadsheet: actuals against budget by department, a forecast for the rest of the year and the variances worth explaining.

Sign convention

The ledger export uses the ledger sign: debit plus, credit minus. The model stores every amount as its contribution to profit: income plus, expense minus. Revenue and cost lines show in display sign, so costs appear as positive amounts. A variance is favourable when it raises profit: more revenue or less cost.

The Power BI report

This page computes the measures of the report in JavaScript from kaldora.json, built by tools/build_replica_data.py through the Python reference. The report also has an Opex page, a department drill-through, a tooltip page and a role Department head that limits each department head to their own cost centres; this replica shows all departments and leaves those out.

Kaldora Facility Services is a fictional company and every number is invented.
